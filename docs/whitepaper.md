## 1. High-Level Abstraction  
The GDk9 Alphabet System treats letters as mathematical objects and their relationships as mappings in a category, a structure where objects are connected by composable mappings with identities. The category is defined as:  
- **Objects**: Alphabet letters (A–Z).  
- **Mappings**: Symmetry relations (idempotent, biphasic, involutive) and directional flows (asymmetric).  
- **Composition**: Mappings compose associatively (if f: X→Y and g: Y→Z, then g o f: X→Z).  
- **Identities**: Each letter X has an identity mapping id_X.  

Substructures called groupoids, where all mappings are invertible, include:  
- **Idempotent groupoid** (x^2 = x): stabilizers.  
- **Biphasic groupoid** (x^2 = f(x)): oscillators.  
- **Involutive groupoid** (x^2 = 1): flips.  
- **Asymmetrics** (x^2 ≠ x, 1): directional flows (not groupoidal).  

Composite mappings, like A→V→W→M, encode transformations: inversion → duplication → integration. Uppercase letters are rigid archetypes (DC cognition), lowercase are flexible alternates (AC cognition), with F→E marking the DC→AC transition.

### 2.2 Symmetry Analysis of the English Alphabet  
Letters are classified by symmetry:  
- **Idempotents** (x^2 = x): A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y (stable under vertical reflection).  
- **Biphasics** (x^2 = f(x)): B, C, D, E, K (stable under horizontal reflection, oscillatory).  
- **Involutives** (x^2 = 1): N, S, Z (180° rotational symmetry, reversible flips).  
- **Asymmetrics** (x^2 ≠ x, 1): F, G, J, L, P, Q, R (broken symmetry, directional flows).  

These classes form a Directed Cognition Graph (DCG), with edges as symmetry relations or asymmetric flows.  

### 2.3 Category-Theoretic Formalization  
The category C is:  
C = (Ob(C), Hom(C), o, id)  
- **Objects**: {A, …, Z}.  
- **Mappings**: Symmetry or asymmetry relations.  
- **Composition**: If f: X→Y and g: Y→Z, then g o f: X→Z.

Groupoids (invertible mappings) include idempotent, biphasic, and involutive substructures. Asymmetric letters form non-invertible mappings, remaining category-only.
